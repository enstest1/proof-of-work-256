## Mechanics / distinctions

### The Gauntlet — art with a verified scar-and-repair history
An independent adversarial swarm challenges an artifact under *predeclared, measurable acceptance rules*. A verified attack result records a defect; a separately verified repair closes it. These paired consequences affect deterministic geometry and remain inspectable. **Do not equate mere oracle panel disagreement with a successful attack.** Suggested pilot: one vulnerable artifact plus a test runner whose artifact hashes and results are admissible in the IMD oracle claim.

### Proof of Causality — why each mark exists
Define a canonical mapping from evidence-backed *typed outcome fields* (e.g., test result counts, accepted repair identifiers, quorum) to visual primitives and historical snapshots. Different inputs must produce reliably distinguishable features. The existing X-Ray role labels are preview explanations, not yet cryptographically validated mark-specific causality. Expose a public evidence manifest for reproducibility.

### The Witness — transparent live work, not synthetic agent thoughts
Display actual IMD work- and review-record events with accurate stage statuses. Stream pending outcomes as **unverified** until the deployment-bound v2 oracle signature is accepted onchain and the Swarm Score finalizes. Replays can be dramatic, but should never fabricate internal thoughts or claim an unaccepted task succeeded.

### Ghost Timelines — competing proposals without losing provenance
Display two work proposals from genuine distinct, inspectable IMD tasks. Predeclare eligibility/evaluation, use an authenticated oracle result to determine qualified branches, and permit collector choice *among eligible branches* with explicit authorization. Save unchosen manifests as offchain pinned archival alternatives, never overwrite immutable onchain history or call an unchosen idea an accepted evolution.

### Symbiosis — two owners, one jointly acquired visual grammar
Build on two-owner verified collaboration: link the same accepted job/evidence to two canvases, derive shared visual elements from a joint root and display pairing mode. Neither collector must surrender an asset. Do not confuse two wallet addresses with guaranteed independent humans.

### The Last Trial — irreversible, earned Masterwork
Specify strict eligibility by unique work classifications, challenge/response, oracle policy, timeouts, and a one-time final-state transition. Avoid user-loss mechanics or scarcity promises. Independent audit required before any mainnet integration.

### The Civilization — one art world made of 256 ownable histories
The Commons becomes a real collaboration graph. Every edge must be traceable to an accepted, unique onchain receipt; each global development must be generated deterministically from verified events. Archetypes can have artistic cultural roles (Orbit/exploration; Vessel/construction; Lattice/networking; Fracture/challenge). A time slider reconstructs historical world-state from a public event log and pinned artifact manifest. Social simulations and autonomous objectives are **research-stage ideas**, not present functionality.

## Prioritized scope guard

**Before October 21 (P0):** Do not add new complex onchain game rules before delivering a qualifying IMD-built/verified NFT, one authentic multi-agent job, an independent contract assessment, a bound oracle attestation, and a verifiable full work→art change. Improve existing Gauntlet/Causality/Witness presentations only where they make that real demonstration clear. See [submission release gates](../docs/HACKATHON-SUBMISSION.md) and [tracked blocking issue](https://github.com/enstest1/proof-of-work-256/issues/3).

**If the authenticity gates pass (P1):** Ghost timelines and aesthetic Symbiosis; improve collector interaction and proof archival.

**After submission (P2):** The Last Trial and an autonomous Civilization; require a separate threat model, data policy, fee model and verified historical reproducibility.

## Preferred long-term synthesis

**PROOF//OF//SURVIVAL:** Gauntlet + Causality + Witness + Last Trial.
**THE LIVING CIVILIZATION:** Civilization + Symbiosis + The Commons + cooperative monuments.
**THE MULTIVERSE:** Ghost Timelines + competing futures + irreversible collector choice.

Collection vision: 256 visually collectible identities with a real, inspectable history of independently checked swarm work, where collaborative actions gradually create one shared artwork. **All originality and effort ratings above are design hypotheses, not empirically validated scores.**

## Flagship strategy — October 10, 2026

- **Flagship A: PROOF//OF//SURVIVAL.** The Gauntlet, Proof of Causality, and The Witness are the core engineering direction; the Last Trial remains post-release until separately assessed. No onchain attack/repair truth is claimed yet.
- **Flagship B: THE LIVING CIVILIZATION.** The world and its four archetypal cultures are now a first-class, explorable site experience; population 256 is a fixed Genesis collection design, not an issued supply. Every proposed social event is marked hypothetical and no invented collaborations are shown as verified.
- **Remaining roadmap:** Ghost Timelines, Symbiosis / paired collector art, irrevocable masterwork mechanics, and full autonomous societal evolution. Their work estimates are hypotheses. No promised airdrops, financial returns, floor prices, artificial scarcity windows, or guaranteed release dates.
- **Actual next engineering gate:** Robinhood Chain **testnet ID 46630** network and deployment validation, independently audited contract, then real wallet-authorized IMD project launch / authentic oracle claim and final Radar submission. A testnet deployment is not a qualifying mainnet IMD launch.
